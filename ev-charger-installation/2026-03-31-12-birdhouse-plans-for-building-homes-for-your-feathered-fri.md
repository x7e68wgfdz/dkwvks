Birdhouses provide shelter for certain types of cavity-nesting birds, offering up a safe space for them to rest. While there are plenty of pre-made birdhouses for purchase, building one yourself—or with the help of a kid or two—is a simple project you can complete in an afternoon. In most cases, all you need is some wood (we recommend using untreated wood so as not to expose birds to potentially harmful chemicals), some nails, and a few basic tools. Following are some of our favorite DIY birdhouse plans for both novice and experienced woodworkers. 1. Simple Birdhouse Photo: The Plans Man via Etsy.com

Looking to create a classic wooden birdhouse with a simple, traditional aesthetic? This is as basic as birdhouse as it gets, and the perfect project for beginner woodworkers. The downloadable plans are accompanied by color photos and provide instructions for both wall-mount and post-mount variations of the project.

Etsy seller Timber Biscuit’s birdhouse can be built by DIY-ers with beginning to intermediate woodworking skills, but if you get stuck you can watch the seller’s tutorial on YouTube. The finished size of the birdhouse is 16 inches x 8 inches by 8.5 inches.
