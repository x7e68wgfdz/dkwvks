If you are thinking about screenshotting a profile that you saw on Hinge, you're probably wondering whether Hinge notifies about screenshots. Hinge does not send notifications about screenshots, whether you want to screen capture their profile or a conversation. This article will teach you everything you need to know about screenshotting on Hinge. Things You Should Know

Hinge does not notify for screenshots of profiles or chat conversations.

Always exercise caution and consider a user's right to privacy before screenshotting on Hinge.

Screenshot on Hinge just as you normally would on your Android or iPhone. Steps Section 1 of 2: Does Hinge notify for screenshots?

Hinge does not notify a user when their profile or message has been screenshotted. Feel free to take as many screenshots as you would like of other people's profiles. They will not receive a notification from Hinge informing them of your screenshot. So go ahead—send that screenshot of your Hinge crush to a friend to see if they think you should pursue a conversation.

[What You Should](https://what-you-should.northlist.xyz/what-you-should/20260826925.html)

It's a good idea to consider the Hinge user's privacy when capturing screenshots of their profile. Avoid sharing the screenshot publicly, at least not without blurring or blocking the user's name or photo.

Sharing screenshots of your chat with friends can be a valid way to receive advice or feedback on what you should say next.
