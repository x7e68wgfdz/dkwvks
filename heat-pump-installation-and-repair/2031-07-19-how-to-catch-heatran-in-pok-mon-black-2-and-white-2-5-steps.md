Heatran is a powerful Fire/Steel legendary Pokémon that first appears in Pokémon Diamond/Pearl/Platinum. It appears in Pokémon Black 2 and White 2, and there is a way to catch it. Steps Download Article 1

Beat the Elite 4. You have to beat the Elite 4 and Champion before you can catch Heatran. 2

Get the Magma Stone. The Magma Stone is located on a cliff on Route 18. You need a Pokémon with Surf to get there. Advertisement 3

Go to the Reversal Mountain cave. Reversal Mountain is located east of Undella Town. 4

From the entrance, go up, right into the next room, down, and right until you find a doctor trainer. There will be a room above him; go into it. If you have already beaten him, you can talk to him to have him heal your Pokémon. 5

Go up to the stairs, and the Magma Stone will activate. Heatran will appear; talk to it to battle it. Advertisement Video Community Q&A  Search Add New Question Question

If I run away from Heatran, can I re-encounter it? Community Answer

No, if you run away from Heatran, you cannot re-encounter it in the same playthrough. To have another chance, you must reload a previous save file from before you initiated the battle.  Thanks! We're glad this was helpful. Thank you for your feedback.
