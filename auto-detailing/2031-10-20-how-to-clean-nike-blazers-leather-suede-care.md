We're here to keep your timeless Nike Blazer Mid ‘77 and Pro sneakers clean and ensure peak performance over time. For the leather accents, it’s safe to use laundry detergent or dish soap and water. To clean the suede accents, however, use a soft cloth, toothbrush, or shoe cleaning brush to gently brush away dirt and grime, and try to avoid getting the area wet. Place a shoe tree or balled-up socks in the toe to keep it from collapsing. Stuff a pair of balled-up socks, a shoe tree, or newspaper inside the toes of your shoes. This can keep the base and the tongue from collapsing while you’re cleaning the outside.[6]XResearch source

Placing something inside the toe can also make it easier to clean all the creases on the outside of the shoe. 3 Clean the leather uppers. Download Article

Gently scrub the leather uppers with and brush, soap, and warm water. Dip a shoe scrubbing brush, toothbrush, or damp cloth in a cleaning solution with warm water 1 tsp (4.93 mL) of laundry detergent or dish soap. Gently scrub the uppers of the shoe in small, circular motions.[7]XExpert SourceChance Dubinick

Alternatively, spray a sneaker cleaner on the leather uppers, dip your brush into clean water, and scrub the shoes in a circular motion to remove dirt.

To tackle hard-to-remove scuffs and stains, gently scrub them with a slightly damp Magic Eraser or apply warm water and a leather-cleaning soap.[9]XResearch source Rub in small circles, then wipe away suds with a dry towel.

After cleaning your shoes, apply a leather conditioner with a soft cloth to prevent the leather from cracking or flaking. Advertisement 4 Brush out the suede accents. Download Article

Brush the suede towards the toe with a dry shoe brush or towel. Using a completely dry brush or a soft towel, brush in the same direction as the material to remove dirt, rocks, and debris. To remove minor scuff marks, brush side-to-side to loosen debris, then brush down towards the toe.[10]XResearch source

Water can cause water stains and damage suede.[11]XResearch source If the suede gets wet during the cleaning process, use a dry brush to go back and forth over the grain and re-fluff the fibers.[12]XResearch source

To remove stains, use a clean suede rubber, pencil eraser, or slightly damp Magic Eraser.[13]XResearch source

For more stubborn stains, dip a cloth in water and white vinegar and scrub the fabric in alternating directions.[14]XResearch source 5 Scrub the soles of your shoes. Download Article

Clean the soles with a soft shoe brush, warm water, and soap. Wet a shoe brush, toothbrush, or washcloth in the warm water soap solution and scrub at any stains or dirt on the rubber midsole and outsole of your shoes.[15]XResearch source Dry the soles with a soft dry cloth.

Alternatively, spray a sneaker cleaner on the soles of your shoes and scrub forcefully with a shoe brush or toothbrush. You can also use a Magic Eraser to scrub stains.[16]XResearch source

Use a toothpick to remove stuck-on mud from the details in the soles as necessary.[17]XResearch source Advertisement 6

Pat your shoes with a cloth and leave them to air dry. Download Article

Blot your shoes with a cloth and place them out of direct sunlight to air dry. After you’ve cleaned your shoes, blot them with a soft cloth or microfiber towel to lift as much soap and dirt from the surface as possible. Place your shoes, laces, and insoles in a warm dry area. Allow your Nike Blazers to air dry completely before wearing them again.[18]XResearch source

Try to avoid rubbing the shoe with the cloth because it can wear down the shoe fabric and spread any remaining dirt.[19]XResearch source

Repeat these steps as necessary or until your drying towel comes away clean.

Consider washing your Nike Blazers about every 2 weeks or more, depending on how often you wear them and where you go when you do.[20]XResearch source Expert Q&A  Search Add New Question Ask a Question 200 characters left
