Photo: Pixel-Shot via Shutterstock. What is a pegboard?

A pegboard is a versatile flat board mounted on a wall that contains evenly spaced perforated holes in a grid-like pattern. You can insert pegs, dowels, hooks, baskets, shelves, and other accessories in the holes to hold tools and other items. Although pegboards were invented in the early 20th century to organize tools in factories and workshops, the Masonite Corporation often receives credit for creating them in 1962, which is when the company introduced the standard perforated hardboard we recognize today. They now show up in homes to display pots and pans in the kitchen; hold hammers and drills in the garage; display collectibles; and organize objects like office supplies, jewelry, and clothing.

Pegboards can be an attractive organizing solution, providing several key benefits. First, they save space in storage areas by using wall space. This helps declutter countertops and floors, and can free up room in drawers and on shelves. Next, they provide convenient access to tools and supplies. With everything you need right in front of you, it is quick and easy to grab the item you need off the wall. Pegboards also offer a great deal of flexibility for organizing your items in various configurations depending on your storage needs. What are some of the best uses for pegboards?

Pegboards provide a simple, handy way to organize a plethora of items. Here are some examples of different ways to incorporate this organizing tool throughout your home. Garage

Pegboards provide an excellent garage storage system, offering quick and easy access to frequently used tools and accessories. Install a pegboard behind a workbench, lining an entire wall, or customized with hooks, shelves, and bins to meet your storage needs. Most tools already have holes in their handles, so they can easily hang on pegboard hooks. Gardening

Use a pegboard in your garage or garden shed to arrange gardening tools and supplies. Add. shelves, wire baskets, or hooks to hold shovels, watering cans, gloves, and other items. Home Office

Keep your desk tidy by creating a hub for office supplies such as pens, scissors, and binder clips, while also showcasing photos, artwork, a calendar, and awards. Kitchen

Just like Julia Child, you can install a pegboard to hold cookware and kitchen gadgets. It will add both convenience and character to your kitchen, while freeing up valuable counter and cabinet space. You can even create your own eye-catching spice rack with a small pegboard. Laundry Room

Hang a large pegboard in your laundry room to organize sorting bins, stain sticks, and an ironing board. Craft Room

Display all your colorful crafting supplies by using a pegboard. This is a great opportunity to keep all those loose tiny items such as beads, paint, brushes, and yarn neatly organized in easily accessible decorative shelves, storage baskets, and bins. Closet
