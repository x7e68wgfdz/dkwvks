Common Rookie Plumbing Mistakes To Avoid

Understanding the most common errors made by novice plumbers can help you prevent damage to your own home’s plumbing system. Here are some of the most common mistakes and how to steer clear of them. Scratching Plumbing Fixtures

One of the most visible and frustrating mistakes is damaging the finish of plumbing fixtures. Chrome faucets and shower heads are particularly susceptible to scratches. This often comes from improper tool use.

To avoid scratching your plumbing fixtures:

Use a strap wrench instead of pliers or a pipe wrench.

Wrap the flexible strap around the fixture securely.

Slowly pull on the handle to turn the fixture.

A strap wrench firmly grips the fixture without the risk of teeth marks or scratches, preserving the appearance of your fixtures.

Pro Tip: Richard Trethewey, This Old House plumbing and heating expert, explains why the strap wrench is the right choice: “It’s a canvas strap. That means we will not scratch the chrome.” After using one on a project, he confirmed, “There’s no teeth marks on the chrome. That’s really handy.” Deforming Pipes

When you’re working with old piping, such as those connected to steam radiators, using the wrong technique can lead to pipe deformation. To avoid bending your pipes, do this:

Make sure the pipe wrench contacts the pipe in three places: the top jaw, bottom jaw, and back of the wrench.

Loosen the wrench slightly to achieve the three-point contact.

Apply force evenly to maintain the pipe’s round shape. Incorrect Fastener Length
